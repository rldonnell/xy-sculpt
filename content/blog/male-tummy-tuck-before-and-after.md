Male tummy tuck results are among the most dramatic in all of plastic surgery, and unlike body contouring procedures, they address something liposuction alone cannot: excess skin and separated abdominal muscles.

## What a Male Tummy Tuck Addresses

A male abdominoplasty (tummy tuck) is typically indicated for men who have:

**Loose or excess abdominal skin**, most commonly after significant weight loss (including post-bariatric patients) or from natural aging and skin laxity that develops even in lean men over time.

**Diastasis recti**, separation of the rectus abdominis muscles down the midline, which creates a soft, rounded abdomen no amount of training can correct. The muscles must be surgically sutured back together.

**Stubborn lower abdominal fat**, often combined with HD VASER liposuction to the flanks and upper abdomen for comprehensive sculpting.

## What Real Before and After Results Look Like

At XYSculptMD, Dr. Moein's male tummy tuck results are defined by:

**Flat, taut abdomen**: The signature outcome, a smooth, flat lower abdominal wall replacing the soft, skin-lax appearance of the pre-operative state.

**Narrowed waistline**: Rectus plication brings the core muscles inward, creating a genuinely narrower mid-torso silhouette.

**Improved clothing fit**: Most patients report dramatic improvements in how trousers and fitted clothing sit, even before final healing.

**Natural-looking scar**: Dr. Moein places the scar low within the natural pubic hairline, in most cases easily hidden by underwear or swimwear.

## Recovery Timeline for Male Tummy Tuck

Recovery from male abdominoplasty is the most demanding of all body contouring procedures, but manageable with proper planning:

Most men take 2 to 3 weeks from work (desk work; longer for physical jobs). Driving is restricted for 10 to 14 days. Compression garment worn full-time for 6 weeks. Light walking encouraged from day 1. No core training for 8 to 10 weeks. Final result, including complete scar maturation, at 12 months.

## Combining Tummy Tuck with Liposuction

The most comprehensive male abdominal transformation combines abdominoplasty with HD VASER liposuction to the flanks and upper abdomen in a single operative session. The result: a flat lower abdomen plus defined lateral contour and a narrow, athletic waistline.
