Male plastic surgery in Los Angeles has undergone a fundamental shift. What was once a niche consideration for high-profile entertainers and executives has become a mainstream pathway for everyday men who want to look as capable as they feel, and who no longer accept the idea that surgical enhancement is off-limits to their gender.

## Why Men Are Choosing Plastic Surgery in 2026

The numbers are striking. Male patients now represent approximately 15 to 20% of all plastic surgery procedures nationwide, a figure that continues to rise annually. In Los Angeles specifically, male body contouring, gynecomastia correction, and facial masculinization have seen sustained year-over-year growth.

The reasons are practical: men live longer, work longer, compete longer. Looking vital at 50 is no longer vanity, it is strategy.

## The Most Popular Male Plastic Surgery Procedures in LA

**Body Contouring and HD Liposuction** remains the most requested male procedure category. Men in LA turn to HD VASER liposuction to address stubborn fat deposits around the abdomen, flanks, chest, and back, areas that remain resistant despite dedicated training.

**Gynecomastia Surgery** is the second most common male procedure. An estimated 30 to 40% of men experience some degree of gynecomastia, yet few seek treatment. LA men are changing that.

**Male Body Contouring Packages**, combining liposuction with skin tightening, abdominoplasty, and chest correction, are increasingly popular among men seeking comprehensive transformation.

## Choosing the Right Surgeon for Male Plastic Surgery in LA

The most important factor is not price, it is specialization. A surgeon who primarily performs female procedures will not produce the same male results as one who exclusively focuses on male anatomy.

Look for: board certification and documented specialization in cosmetic and body-contouring surgery, documented experience with male-specific procedures, a before-and-after gallery that is primarily or exclusively male, and a consultation that feels tailored to your concerns.
