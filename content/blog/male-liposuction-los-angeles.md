When men decide to address stubborn fat deposits in Los Angeles, the question is rarely if, it is which type of liposuction. The difference between traditional liposuction and HD VASER liposuction is not merely technical; it is the difference between fat removal and body sculpting.

## What Is Traditional Liposuction?

Traditional tumescent liposuction uses mechanical cannula movement and suction to remove fat. It is effective at volume reduction but works indiscriminately, it cannot selectively preserve fat around muscle insertions or safely address the fibrous fat of the male back and chest.

## What Is HD VASER Liposuction?

VASER (Vibration Amplification of Sound Energy at Resonance) uses ultrasonic energy to emulsify fat before removal. This precision enables six-pack etching, chest definition, and back sculpting that traditional liposuction cannot safely achieve.

## Why HD VASER Matters Specifically for Men

Male fat distribution differs fundamentally from female fat distribution. Male fat is denser, more fibrous (particularly in the flanks, back, and chest), and organized differently around muscle groups. VASER was designed for these characteristics.
