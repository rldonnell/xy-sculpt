Six-pack surgery, technically called abdominal etching or high-definition VASER liposuction, is one of the most sought-after male plastic surgery procedures in Los Angeles. If you have ever trained seriously and wondered why your abs remain hidden beneath a thin layer of fat, this is the procedure that changes everything.

## What Is Six-Pack Surgery?

Six-pack surgery uses HD VASER ultrasonic liposuction to selectively remove fat in the areas between and around the rectus abdominis muscle groups, not just fat removal, but precision sculpting that follows the anatomical lines of the underlying musculature.

The result is visible abdominal definition, the appearance of a "six-pack", that persists as long as the patient maintains a stable weight.

## Who Is a Candidate for Ab Etching?

The ideal candidate for six-pack surgery is a man who:

**Already has reasonable muscle development**: Ab etching reveals what is there, it does not create muscle where none exists. Men who train regularly and have a functional core are optimal candidates.

**Is at or near their ideal weight**: Best results occur in men with a BMI under 28 and a thin layer of subcutaneous fat. Men with visceral (deep belly) fat are poor candidates for etching.

**Has realistic expectations**: Ab etching creates visible definition. It will not produce the extreme striations of a competitive bodybuilder, but for most men, the results are more visible definition than they have ever seen.

## What Does Six-Pack Surgery Cost in Los Angeles?

## Recovery from Ab Etching

Most men return to desk work within 5 to 7 days. Compression garment worn 24/7 for 4 to 6 weeks. Light walking from day 1. No ab training for 4 to 6 weeks. Swelling resolves progressively; visible etching definition appears at 6 to 8 weeks and improves through 6 months.
