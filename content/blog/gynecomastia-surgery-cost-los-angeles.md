Gynecomastia surgery in Los Angeles ranges from approximately $4,500 to $12,000+ depending on the grade of gynecomastia, technique required, and facility. Here is a transparent breakdown of what drives cost, and how to evaluate whether you are getting fair value.

## What Drives Gynecomastia Surgery Cost

**Grade and Technique**: Grade I gynecomastia (small glandular button, no excess skin) may require only periareolar excision, a shorter, simpler procedure. Grade III or IV with significant skin laxity and drooping may require extended incisions, skin removal, and free nipple grafting, a substantially more complex surgery.

**VASER Liposuction Component**: Most gynecomastia cases have both a fatty and a glandular component. Cases requiring VASER liposuction in addition to gland excision cost more than excision alone, but produce dramatically better chest contour.

**Surgeon's Experience**: Board-certified surgeons who specialize in male chest surgery command premium fees, and produce premium results. Bargain pricing often reflects limited experience or simplified technique.

**Surgical Facility**: Accredited outpatient surgical centers cost less than hospital-based ORs but still provide full anesthesia support. "Office suite" procedures at discounted prices sacrifice facility oversight.
