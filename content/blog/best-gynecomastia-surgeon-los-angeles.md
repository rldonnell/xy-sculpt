Gynecomastia surgery requires a specific combination of surgical skill, aesthetic judgment, and male-focused expertise that not every plastic surgeon in Los Angeles possesses. Here is how to identify the best surgeon for your specific case.

## Non-Negotiable Credentials

**Board Certification**: Your gynecomastia surgeon should be a board-certified surgeon with specific training and experience in cosmetic and body-contouring procedures. Verify the certification, and, just as important, the surgeon's focus. General credentials alone don't guarantee male-chest expertise.

**Specialty Focus**: Prioritize a surgeon whose practice centers on cosmetic and body-contouring surgery, with a high volume of male chest procedures. Focused, male-specific experience matters more than a long list of general credentials, in a field where anyone with a medical degree can technically perform surgical procedures.

**Hospital Privileges**: A surgeon who has privileges at an accredited surgical facility is subject to ongoing peer review. Surgeons who operate only in unlicensed "suites" have no such oversight.

## Gynecomastia-Specific Experience

Gynecomastia surgery is not simply liposuction. Glandular gynecomastia requires actual tissue excision, a skill distinct from fat removal. A surgeon with 20 general plastic surgery cases and 2 gynecomastia cases is not equivalent to one with hundreds of male chest procedures.

**Ask specifically**: How many gynecomastia cases do you perform per year? What percentage of your practice is male patients? Do you treat all grades (I to IV)?

## The Male-Specific Factor

This is where most LA plastic surgeons fall short. The majority of plastic surgery practices are female-focused by patient volume, breast augmentation, tummy tucks, mommy makeovers. A surgeon who treats male patients as a side category has less calibrated judgment about male chest aesthetics.

Dr. Moein at XYSculptMD operates exclusively on male patients, meaning every aesthetic judgment, every technique refinement, and every result standard is calibrated specifically to the male chest.

## Red Flags to Avoid

Avoid surgeons who: quote a price without examining you, cannot show you male-specific before and after photos, do not discuss technique differences (gland excision vs. liposuction-only), or pressure you to book on the same day as your consultation.
