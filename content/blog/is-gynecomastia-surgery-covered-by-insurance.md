Gynecomastia surgery is one of the most common questions men ask about insurance coverage. The answer is nuanced, and getting it right can save you thousands of dollars.

## The Short Answer

**Gynecomastia surgery is sometimes covered by insurance, but usually not for cosmetic reasons.** Coverage depends on whether your insurer classifies the condition as medically necessary.

## When Insurance Covers Gynecomastia Surgery

Insurance carriers (including Aetna, Blue Shield, UnitedHealthcare, and others) may cover gynecomastia surgery when the condition causes:

**Physical symptoms**: Breast tenderness or pain, skin breakdown or rash beneath breast tissue, or interference with daily activities.

**Psychological distress with documented history**: Some carriers require psychiatric documentation showing significant psychological impact of the condition.

**True glandular gynecomastia (not pseudogynecomastia)**: Insurance typically requires confirmation that the enlargement is glandular tissue, not simply excess fat from weight gain. Dr. Moein can document this with clinical examination and appropriate imaging.

## When Insurance Does Not Cover It

If your gynecomastia is purely cosmetic, meaning it does not cause physical pain or dysfunction, most insurance plans will classify it as an elective cosmetic procedure and decline coverage.

**Pseudogynecomastia** (fat deposits in the chest from obesity, not glandular tissue) is almost never covered by insurance.
